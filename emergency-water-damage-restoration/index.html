Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Westchester

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.

Why Emergency Water Damage Restoration Is Not a DIY Job

In an emergency, the hard part is not mopping up what you can see. It is knowing where the rest of the water went. Water runs under baseboards, into wall cavities and beneath flooring within minutes. Meters and thermal imaging show us the wet spots hidden from view, which means they get handled that night rather than surfacing weeks on as a stain or a smell.

Our night crew arrives with gear homes simply do not have, including commercial air movers, properly sized dehumidifiers and truck-mounted extractors. The crew is trained to stay safe near contaminated water and wet wiring, and from hour one it records the loss in photos and moisture readings. That record helps when you file a claim. You get one team that handles the emergency and stays with the job until the home is dry.
