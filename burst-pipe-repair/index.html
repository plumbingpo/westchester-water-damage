Home / Services / Burst Pipe Repair

A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Westchester Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Westchester and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
