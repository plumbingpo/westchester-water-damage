How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
